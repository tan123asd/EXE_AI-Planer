Delete Your ZENTRO Account

Deleting your account removes your ZENTRO profile and associated data (like tasks, schedules, expenses, and settings). Review what will be removed, then confirm deletion. Permanent deletion starts processing after you submit your request.

FAQ

What happens next?

We remove your data in stages and complete deletion within up to 30 days. While processing, your data may be temporarily unavailable.

Processing window

Deletion completed within 30 days (max).

Irreversible

Once confirmed, deletion is permanent.

Tip: If you’re unsure, you can also choose to pause your subscription inside the app.

Data Deletion Information

When you delete your ZENTRO account, we remove the following data types associated with your profile.

  • User profile

    Account details tied to your identity and profile information.

  • Tasks and deadlines

    Your study tasks, reminders, and deadline-related information.

  • AI schedules

    AI-generated schedules and any related scheduling data.

  • Expense tracking data

    Spending and budgeting entries associated with your account.

  • Preferences & settings

    Notification preferences, productivity settings, and account configuration.

  • AI chat context

    Any stored conversation or context used to support your planning experience.

Retention Policy

Data removal within 30 days

After you confirm deletion, ZENTRO begins the process to remove your data. Deletion is completed within up to 30 days.

Irreversible deletion warning

Account deletion is permanent. Once confirmed, you will not be able to recover your data, tasks, schedules, or settings.
